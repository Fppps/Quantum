Welcome to Quantum Particle Map

This is a hardware-accelerated physics engine calculating continuous probability logic and interactions. Here is how to use the map:

  • Navigation: Use your scroll wheel to zoom in and out of the universe. Click and drag to pan the camera around the map.
  • Time Control: Use the Time Dilate slider to run the universe in extreme slow-motion or fast-forward. Use the Timeline Scrub to physically travel back in time.
  • The Physics:
    Protons (+) Anchor elements. Attract electrons and neutrons, repel each other.
    Electrons (-) Buzz wildly. Attracted to protons, repel electrons.
    Neutrons (0) Nuclear glue. They cluster tightly to hold structures together.
    Watch for Photons—high energy flashes of light emitted when particles clash violently!
  • Draw Modes:
    Trails: Visualizes the historic quantum strings.
    Clutter: Maps out dense areas in thermal color.
    Entropy: Maps the kinetic energy (velocity) of the elements. Cold is purple, hot/fast is bright pink.
