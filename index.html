Welcome to Quantum Particle Map

This is a hardware-accelerated physics engine calculating continuous probability logic and interactions.

  • Navigation: Use your scroll wheel to zoom in/out. Click and drag to pan around.
  • Time Control: Use the Time Dilate slider in the Time & Controls tab to slow down or speed up logic. Use Timeline Scrub to travel back in time.
  • The Physics:
    Protons (+) Anchor elements. Attract electrons and neutrons.
    Electrons (-) Buzz wildly. Attracted to protons, repel electrons.
    Neutrons (0) Nuclear glue. Hold structures together.
    Watch for Photons—flashes of light emitted during violent clashes!
    Critical Mass: If too many protons and neutrons clump together, the atom becomes unstable and violently undergoes fission!
  • Draw Modes:
    Trails: Visualizes historic strings.
    Clutter: Maps out dense areas in thermal color.
    Entropy: Maps kinetic energy (Cold=purple, Hot=pink).
